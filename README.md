# MSc Econometrics

Python exercises and datasets for the MSc Econometrics tutorials.

The complete student material is in [Notebooks](Notebooks/), organised into tutorial subfolders. Download this repository as a ZIP and extract it, then upload the enclosed `Notebooks` folder to Google Drive > My Drive.

Open a notebook with Google Colab and run all cells, starting with **Environment setup**. Authorize Google Drive access when prompted. See [the notebook instructions](Notebooks/README.md) for other folder locations and local Jupyter use.

## Tutorials

- [Financial Returns](Notebooks/Financial_Returns/): prices and returns, rolling equity premium, and tail risk / VaR.

- [OLS](Notebooks/OLS/): simple and multiple regression of energy-sector returns on the market and Brent; heteroskedasticity and robust inference; Monte Carlo simulation; rolling CAPM; and Okun's law with linear, quadratic and rolling regressions.

- [Time Series (Tutorial V)](Notebooks/Time_Series/): autocorrelations and the Ljung-Box test for daily US market returns in 2021-2025; GDP growth dynamics in the United States, Germany and Italy; AR order selection, persistence and forecasting.

Each tutorial includes its data alongside the notebooks.
